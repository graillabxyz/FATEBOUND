# Shared OMNIPATH collection

The alpha contains six Legends, sixty global Cards and twelve current Omens. Cards are never grouped into Legend-owned pools. `cardCompatible` evaluates the Legend's Affinity array against a recursive requirement: one Affinity, `anyOf`, `allOf`, nested combinations, or no requirement (Unbound). Empty requirement groups fail closed. Mechanical tags do not affect equipping.

The prototype Affinities are Might, Guile, Wisdom, Wild, Spirit, Shadow, Order and Chaos. Names, descriptions and neutral symbols live in `content/affinities.ts`. They are not colors or RPG classes. The six Legends access 29–39 Cards each, with 209 legal Legend/Card pairs and 324,232 legal four-Card combinations in total.

Rarities are 29 Common, 18 Uncommon, 9 Rare and 4 Mythic. Rarity never multiplies damage, utility or AI score. Full Card data includes set, collector number, timing, requirements, effects, text, balance metadata and explicit persistence. All current Cards have persistence none.

Fresh profiles own Basajaun and Anansi, sixteen shared Cards, six Omens and 250 Coins. Their curated Hands are editable examples, not separate Card pools. Legacy profiles retain prior entitlements through explicit ID migration; new players do not receive the full collection. Current starter Omens always include one signature plus two numbered Omens. Six signature designs exist, all shared; a Legend's recommendation is not an ownership restriction.

Local two-Card packs save outcomes and grants before animation. A pending receipt resumes after reload, is charged once and reveals each Card separately. Ownership and equipped selection are separate. Earn packs through match milestones, Mastery and the free Season Path, or spend earned Coins. Cards can also be acquired individually with Coins, including Mythics. This device-local alpha economy is not an authenticated server marketplace and has no real-money pack sales.

The collection supports Affinity, Unbound, ownership, rarity and timing filters. Loadout building defaults to legal Cards and explains incompatible requirements. AND/OR is written explicitly rather than encoded solely by color. The internal metrics simulation page evaluates every legal Legend/Card pair, exact face distributions and sampled complete Hands. Its approximate utility model includes damage, Ward, healing, Focus, manipulation, denial, Life costs, accessibility and activation probability; human playtests remain necessary.
